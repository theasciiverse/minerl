MineRL
======

Whales' side-view mining roguelike (2014), finished for Asciiverse. Dig
down through dirt and stone for coal, iron, copper, silver, gold, rubies
and diamonds, sell them at the shop on the surface, buy better tools, and
go deeper, until you find the Heartstone at the bottom of the claim and
carry it up into the daylight. Cockroaches, worms, ladder-eating termites
and ore-thieving goblins live down there, falls hurt, boulders come down,
and when your stamina runs out every step costs health.

This build is the Asciiverse edition of github.com/Whales/minerl, from
github.com/theasciiverse/minerl; PATCHES.md there lists every change.

Running it
----------

Start ./minerl in a terminal of at least 80x24. It reads its screens from
cuss/ beside the program. Your claim and your scores are saved in
$XDG_STATE_HOME/minerl (usually ~/.local/state/minerl), or in
~/Library/Application Support/minerl on macOS.

Arrows or hjkl walk and dig, a at the shop sells and buys, w ladder,
s support, r rope, f crossbow, d dynamite, ? help, S save and quit.
